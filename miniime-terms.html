2. What MiniiMe Does

MiniiMe is a personal journal that lets you:

  • Write, film or talk memories, including a daily 30-second recap (Daily 30)
  • Seal time capsules that stay locked until the day you choose
  • Attach photos, videos and a song to a day
  • Design a pixel MiniiMe, pick the places you've lived, and add the people and pets in your life
  • Swipe through My Life, an animated chapter for each year
  • Talk to your MiniiMe from any year, using AI built from what you wrote and said back then
  • Add friends and share chosen memories into each other's timelines
  • Back up your journal privately and restore it on a new phone

3. Accounts

  • Give accurate sign-in information and don't create accounts for other people without their permission
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Account > Delete account

4. Free Month and MiniiMe Yearly

  • Every new MiniiMe account gets its first month free, counted from when the account is created
  • After that, MiniiMe Yearly ($14.99 per year in the US, or the local price shown in the App Store) keeps your MiniiMe chats, life chapter summaries and cloud backup going
  • MiniiMe Yearly is an auto-renewing yearly subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase
  • It renews automatically at the price shown unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period
  • Manage or cancel any time in your Apple Account settings. Cancelling stops future renewals; the current period stays active until it ends
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies. If Apple refunds or revokes a purchase, the plan it paid for ends
  • If your family uses Ask to Buy, a purchase is completed only after the family organizer approves it
  • Without a plan, your journal still stays on your iPhone, keeps working for writing and recording, and an existing backup can still be restored

We may change prices or what's included in a plan. Apple will tell you about price changes to an existing subscription as its rules require.
