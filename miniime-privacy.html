MiniiMe Privacy Policy

A cozy pixel journal where you can talk to past you

Effective Date: October 25, 2026
The short version: Your journal lives on your iPhone and is backed up privately so you can get it back on a new phone. Memories are only sent to our AI partner (OpenAI) if you agree, and only to write your MiniiMe replies, titles and chapter summaries. Friends see only what you choose to share. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

1. Who We Are

MiniiMe is made by Colin Boykin ("we", "us"). This policy explains what MiniiMe collects, why, where it goes, and the choices you have. It applies to the MiniiMe iPhone app and the MiniiMe servers it talks to.

2. What Stays on Your iPhone

Everything you put into MiniiMe is stored on your device first, including:

  • Memories you write, record or talk (Daily 30s, video memories, time capsules), plus their tags, dates, titles and summaries
  • Text versions of what you say in your videos. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone using Apple's speech recognition when your phone supports it
  • Your pixel MiniiMe, the places you've lived, and the people and pets you add to My Life
  • Songs you pick for a day, streaks, ranks, badges and app settings
  • A "voice profile" measured on your phone from your own memories (things like sentence length, punctuation and favorite phrases) so your MiniiMes sound like you
  • Your optional app passcode and security answers. These are never stored as written: only salted, one-way hashes are kept in your iPhone's Keychain, and they are never backed up or sent to us

Photos and videos from your library: When MiniiMe has full access to your photo library, an attached photo or video is saved as a link to the item in your library (plus a small thumbnail), so it isn't copied or uploaded. Without full access, a copy is saved in MiniiMe and backed up like your other recordings.

3. Private Cloud Backup

So you don't lose your journal, MiniiMe backs up your memories to our servers on Google Cloud (Firestore and Cloud Storage, in the United States). The backup includes:

  • The text of your memories, video transcripts, titles, summaries, tags and songs
  • Videos you record in MiniiMe and any photos or videos copied into the app (videos are compressed on your phone before upload)
  • A random backup ID that links the backup to your install or account

Backups are only readable by your account and are used only to restore your journal after a reinstall or on a new phone. Sealed time capsules are backed up too, but stay sealed in the app until their date. You can turn backup off at any time in Settings.

4. Your Account

Signing in is optional for journaling. If you create an account, you sign in with an email address or phone number and a password through Google Firebase Authentication. Phone numbers are used only as a sign-in name; we don't send text messages. An account lets your backup follow you to a new phone and is required to talk to your MiniiMes, because that uses AI on our servers.

5. AI Features (Only With Your OK)

Before anything is sent, MiniiMe shows a screen explaining what is shared and with whom, and asks for your permission. You can change this at any time in Settings > AI & Privacy.

When AI is on, the memories that are relevant to a request, your voice profile, and the names of people in your story are sent through our server to OpenAI to:

  • Write replies when you talk to your MiniiMe from a past year
  • Write My Life chapter summaries
  • Write titles and short summaries for memories and videos

If a friend shared a memory with you and it's part of a conversation, that shared memory may be included as well. Sealed time capsules are never sent. Our OpenAI key stays on our server, never in the app. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train their models; OpenAI may keep it for a limited time for abuse monitoring, as described in their own privacy policy. We keep a count of how many chat messages you send each day to prevent abuse, not the messages themselves.

6. Friends and Shared Memories

Friends on MiniiMe is small and private, not a social network:

  • You add someone with their friend code, and you're only friends once both of you say yes
  • Friends see only your name, your pixel MiniiMe, and the memories you choose to share with them
  • A shared memory appears in a friend's timeline only if they accept it, and you can take it back at any time
  • A shared memory's song, photos and videos are shown to your friend through short-lived, view-only links to your private backup. Nothing is copied into their backup, and the links stop working if you unfriend them or take the memory back. Photos may be cached on their phone so they load quickly, and that cache is cleared when the memory is taken back
  • Friend notifications never include what a memory says, so nothing private shows on a lock screen

7. Songs

When you search Apple Music in the app, only your search words are sent, directly to Apple. If you paste a YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify or Apple Music link, MiniiMe asks that service for the song's name, and the song plays in the service's own player only when you tap play. Those services may collect information under their own privacy policies when their player loads.

8. Anonymous Usage Counts

MiniiMe can send simple counts of which features get used (for example, how many Daily 30s are filmed versus typed). These are event names and small labels only, never anything you wrote, said or recorded, and are not used for advertising or tracking. You can turn them off in Settings > AI & Privacy.

9. Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings, we receive your message along with your account email (if any), app version and device model so we can follow up and fix problems. Only we read it.

10. Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. To confirm your MiniiMe Yearly plan, the app sends your App Store transaction information to our server, which checks it with Apple. We also note when your account was created so we know when your free first month ends.

11. Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: only when you choose to record a video or talk in a Daily 30
  • Speech recognition: to turn what you say into text on your iPhone
  • Photos: to attach photos and videos you pick
  • Notifications: for your Daily 30 reminder, time capsules that open, and friend requests or shared memories
  • Apple Music (optional): to play full songs if you subscribe to Apple Music

MiniiMe does not access your contacts or location.

12. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell or rent your data, and we don't use advertising networks. We share data only with the service providers needed to run MiniiMe:

  • Google Cloud and Firebase: hosting, backup storage and sign-in
  • OpenAI: AI replies, titles and summaries, only when you've agreed
  • Apple: purchases, notifications, speech recognition and Apple Music
  • Friends you choose: only the memories you share with them

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

13. Security

  • All communication with our servers uses HTTPS encryption
  • Your sign-in session is kept in your iPhone's Keychain
  • Your backup is tied to your account, and shared media is only reachable through short-lived links
  • An optional passcode (and Face ID) can lock the app, and the journal is hidden in the app switcher

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

14. How Long We Keep Data

Your backup is kept as long as you have backup on or keep your account. When you delete a memory, it's removed from your backup too. Daily usage counters used for rate limits expire after a few days. When you delete your account, your backup and friend data are deleted from our servers.

15. Your Choices and Rights

  • Delete memories: any time, in the app
  • Turn off AI or usage counts: Settings > AI & Privacy
  • Turn off backup: in Settings
  • Delete your account: Settings > Account > Delete account. This deletes your sign-in, your cloud backup (text, videos and photos), your friend code, friendships and anything you've shared. Memories on your iPhone stay unless you delete them or the app
  • Access or other requests: email us and we'll help, including requests under laws like the GDPR or CCPA

16. Children

MiniiMe is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it. Purchases made through Apple's Ask to Buy need a parent or guardian's approval.

17. Changes to This Policy

We'll post any changes on this page and update the effective date. For material changes, we'll also let you know in the app.
